embedder (parent origin) — stands in for itch.io

The frame below is the game, served from a different origin. That is the whole point: on itch.io the player is on yourname.itch.io while the WebGL build is served from an itch.zone host, so Google sees a third-party iframe. One Tap needs the publickey-credentials-get permission, which a cross-origin frame does not get unless the embedder delegates it with allow= — and itch.io is not going to delegate anything on our behalf.

Use this to answer one question: does our fallback button still work when One Tap is denied? If the frame shows a Google button and its popup opens, the fix is good. If it does not, web play cannot do Google sign-in and the docs need to say so plainly.
